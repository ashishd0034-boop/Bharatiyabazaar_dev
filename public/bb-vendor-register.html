Step 3 — Vendor Agreement

Please read and accept the following terms · कृपया निम्नलिखित शर्तें पढ़ें और स्वीकार करें

1. Platform Margin

The platform margin is fixed for your business category and is non-negotiable. Current margins range from 7% to 25% depending on category. Margins are configurable by the platform admin and may be updated. If updated, you will be notified and can choose whether to continue.

2. Settlement Cycle

  • Settlements are processed weekly on Mondays for the previous week (Monday–Sunday).
  • Settlement amount = Gross sales − Platform margin − Admin charge − TDS (if applicable).
  • Early settlement (before Monday) incurs a flat Rs.250 fee.

3. Admin Charges

  • Bank transfer settlement: 10% of post-margin amount.
  • Wallet settlement: 5% of post-margin amount.
  • Volume discount tiers reduce the admin charge (not the vendor margin).
  • Current tiers: 0% · 10% · 20% · 30% · 50% discount on admin charge.

4. Tax Deduction (TDS)

  • Settlement payouts: TDS under Section 194C. Threshold: Rs.30,000 single payment OR Rs.1,00,000 aggregate per FY. Rate: 1% (individual) / 2% (company/firm) / 20% (no PAN).
  • Vendor referral bonus: TDS under Section 194H. Threshold: Rs.20,000 per FY. Rate: 3% (with PAN) / 20% (no PAN).
  • Form 16A is issued quarterly for TDS deducted.

5. Security Deposit

  • A refundable security deposit of Rs.5,000 is required at activation.
  • The deposit is frozen when your wallet balance falls below Rs.500.
  • In case of fraud deactivation, pending member commissions are covered from the deposit first, then penalties, then remainder is forfeited.

6. Inactivity

  • Vendor accounts become INACTIVE after 31 days of no activity.
  • Vendor accounts become FROZEN after 91 days of continued inactivity.
  • Vendor accounts are CLOSED after 181 days of continued inactivity.
  • Upon closure, the position is inherited by the platform and commissions go to the company wallet.

7. Vendor Referral Bonus

  • You earn 0.25% of ALL sales at vendors you refer — for their lifetime on the platform.
  • Only ONE referrer per vendor — the first recorded referral wins permanently.
  • Referral bonus is subject to TDS under Section 194H.

8. Fraud & Penalties

  • Fraud: 10x transaction value penalty + permanent deactivation.
  • Data tampering: 5x transaction value penalty.
  • QR refusal: Rs.1,000 per incident.
  • Member commissions are covered from security deposit before penalty forfeiture.
