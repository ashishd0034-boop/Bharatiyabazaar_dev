1. Security Deposit Mode & Policy

  • Mandatory Refundable Deposit: ₹5,000 (500,000 paise) recorded at platform onboarding.
  • Deposit Protection: Protects member commissions and covers settlement reconciliation.
  • Freeze Threshold: If your settlement wallet falls below ₹500, deposit freeze state is engaged until replenished.

2. Settlement Cycle & TDS Compliance

  • Weekly Settlement: Every Monday at 00:00 UTC for previous week's cleared sales.
  • TDS Section 194C: 1% (Individual) / 2% (Company) on gross sales exceeding ₹30k single or ₹1L aggregate.
  • On-Demand Early Settlement: Available at any time with flat ₹250 administrative fee.

3. Inactivity Lifecycle

  • 31 Days Inactive: Account marked INACTIVE if no sales recorded.
  • 91 Days Inactive: Account marked FROZEN; settlements halted.
  • 181 Days Inactive: Account permanently CLOSED.
