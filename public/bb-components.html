UI/UX Rules

Do

  • Show exact deduction breakdown before every payout.
  • Show why an amount is locked or pending.
  • Use Indian number formatting: Rs.1,00,000.
  • Use empty states to guide the next action.
  • Show ID type on every earning row: MAIN / SUB / REBIRTH.
  • Use confirmation modals for withdrawals and admin changes.

Do Not

  • Do not show pending amounts as withdrawable.
  • Do not imply rebirth IDs have MY SYSTEM or ACB.
  • Do not say “all earnings withdrawable” without source checks.
  • Do not show Sunday waiver.
  • Do not hardcode TDS without threshold logic.
  • Do not mix vendor settlement 194C with member 194H.
