How Vouchers Work

1
Receive
Earn a Rs.200 voucher when your AutoPool completes Level 5, Level 6 and Level 7.
2
Store
Vouchers stay in your Voucher Wallet with a hard expiry of exactly 1 year from issue date. No grace period. No replacement.
3
Redeem
Use them on purchases across Bharatiya Bazaar partner stores and vendors.
Vouchers are non-transferable and cannot be converted back to cash. Unused vouchers are forfeited at hard expiry.
Section 194R: If your aggregate voucher face value crosses Rs.20,000 in a financial year, TDS liability = 10% of the full aggregate voucher value. The liability is recovered automatically from your next withdrawal and is NOT reversed if a voucher expires unused.
