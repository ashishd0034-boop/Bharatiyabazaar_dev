Earn through network growth, referral commissions, and shopping rewards — all from one platform.

Anant Samriddhi Chakra

AutoPool · 8-Level Global Binary Pool

Every member is automatically placed in the global AutoPool using breadth-first positioning. As your subtree fills, you earn cash and generate rebirth IDs.

  • Rs.800 cash per complete pool cycle (Levels 1–3)
  • 4 rebirth IDs generated at Levels 4–7
  • Rs.600 in product vouchers at Levels 5, 6, 7
  • Rebirth IDs continue earning in the global AutoPool
  • No manual placement — fully automatic
🤝

MY SYSTEM

Referral Network · Levels 1–3 Pay Cash

Build your personal binary referral tree. Your sponsor places you LEFT or RIGHT. Refer 2 members to achieve ACB status and unlock withdrawal.

  • Rs.300 at Level 1 · Rs.300 at Level 2 · Rs.200 at Level 3
  • ACB achieved with 2 direct referrals (1 LEFT + 1 RIGHT)
  • 7-day validity hold on all MY SYSTEM commissions
  • Pay-Once Rule: Level 1–3 cash paid only once per ID
  • Applies to all purchased IDs (MAIN + SUB)
🛒

Setu Kosh

Shopping Rewards · 10-Level Binary Tree

Shop at partner vendors and earn commissions through a separate 10-level global tree. Every Rs.1,000 of shopping creates a Setu Kosh ID.

  • Commissions funded by vendor margin
  • 10-level upline commission flow
  • Referral bonus: 0.25% of purchase amount
  • No ACB required for Setu Kosh earnings
  • Separate tree from AutoPool and MY SYSTEM

Achieve ACB status by referring 2 direct members — 1 on LEFT and 1 on RIGHT — in your MY SYSTEM tree. ACB unlocks withdrawal access for your AutoPool and MY SYSTEM earnings. Setu Kosh earnings do not require ACB.

Every purchased ID can independently achieve ACB. Rebirth IDs do not get ACB status.
