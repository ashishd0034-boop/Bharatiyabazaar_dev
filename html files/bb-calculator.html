Understanding TDS on Bharatiya Bazaar Earnings

Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is a mandatory deduction under the Indian Income Tax Act. Bharatiya Bazaar deducts TDS on eligible earnings before payout and deposits it with the Income Tax Department on your behalf.

Section 194H — Cash Commissions

  • Applicable to AutoPool cash, MY SYSTEM cash, and Setu Kosh cash commissions
  • Threshold: Rs.20,000 aggregate per financial year
  • Rate with PAN: 3%
  • Rate without PAN: 20%
  • Deduction timing: withdrawal request, finalised on COMPLETED withdrawal
  • Certificate: Form 16A, quarterly

Section 194R — Product Vouchers

  • Applicable to Rs.200 vouchers issued at AutoPool Levels 5, 6 and 7
  • Threshold: Rs.20,000 aggregate voucher face value per financial year
  • Rate: 10%
  • Once threshold crosses, liability = 10% of full aggregate voucher value
  • Recovered from next withdrawal automatically
  • Not reversed if voucher expires unused

Admin Charge — Not TDS

  • Bank transfer: 10% of post-TDS amount
  • Member wallet transfer: 5% of post-TDS amount
  • This is a platform fee, not a tax
  • Not deposited with the Income Tax Department
  • Shown separately in wallet statement

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to file ITR for Bharatiya Bazaar earnings? Yes, if your total income exceeds the basic exemption limit. TDS deducted by Bharatiya Bazaar is reflected in Form 26AS and can be claimed as credit when filing your ITR.
What if I do not have a PAN card? Without PAN, TDS is deducted at 20% instead of 3%. Submit your PAN through KYC Tier 2 to use the lower rate.
When will I get my TDS certificate? Form 16A is generated quarterly and made available under KYC & Documents for members who had TDS deducted during that quarter.
Are vouchers taxable? Yes. Vouchers are covered under Section 194R. Once aggregate voucher face value crosses Rs.20,000 in a financial year, liability is calculated as 10% of the full aggregate voucher value.
What is the difference between TDS and admin charge? TDS is a government tax deposited with the Income Tax Department. Admin charge is a platform fee retained by Bharatiya Bazaar. Both appear separately in the payout breakdown.
