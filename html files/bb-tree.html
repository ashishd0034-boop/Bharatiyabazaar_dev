How MY SYSTEM Works

1
Sponsor-decided placement for new members
Your sponsor selects your placement (LEFT or RIGHT) before sending the registration invite. You cannot change it during registration.
2
SUB IDs auto-placed under MAIN ID
If you purchase multiple IDs, the first is your MAIN ID. Additional SUB IDs are automatically placed under your MAIN ID in breadth-first order.
3
Later additions can be manually placed
If you add extra IDs or members later, you may choose an open position in your MY SYSTEM structure. If no position is selected, the system uses the next available breadth-first position.
4
Cash pays only Levels 1–3
Rs.800 total is the maximum cash payout from MY SYSTEM. Levels 4 and below do not generate MY SYSTEM income.
5
ACB unlock
2 direct referrals (1 Left + 1 Right) achieves ACB status. ACB unlocks withdrawal access for that ID's earnings.
6
MY SYSTEM applies to all purchased IDs
Every purchased ID (MAIN and SUB) can earn MY SYSTEM commissions and achieve ACB. Rebirth IDs do NOT participate in MY SYSTEM.
