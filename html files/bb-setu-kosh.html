How Setu Kosh Works

1
Shop at Partner Vendors
Visit any partner vendor and scan their QR code using the Bharatiya Bazaar app.
2
Counter Accumulates
Every purchase adds to your member-level ₹1,000 counter across all vendors. Purchases through MAIN, SUB, or REBIRTH IDs all count toward the same member counter.
3
ID Created at ₹1,000
When the counter reaches ₹1,000, a Setu Kosh ID is auto-placed in the global 10-level binary commission tree using breadth-first placement.
4
Earn Network Commissions
Upline members earn commissions from your purchases across 10 levels. You earn from members you referred. Commissions become CONFIRMED after vendor weekly settlement.
The Setu Kosh tree is completely separate from the AutoPool tree and MY SYSTEM tree. Your MY SYSTEM sponsor is identified for referral bonus only, not for Setu Kosh tree placement.
